## The first ten minutes

Someone falls in the kitchen and can't reach the phone. A field catches in
August. A man in a hi-vis vest talks his way into a house, saying he's come to
read the meter.

The health centre is eleven kilometres away, the hospital forty-seven, and one
Guardia Civil post covers dozens of villages like this one. They will come, and
they are always called first — but somebody arrives before them, and that
somebody is a neighbour.

**In those first minutes, how does a village respond on purpose rather than by
luck?**

## What it is

- **[The Red Escudo]({{< relref "/how-it-works/the-red-escudo" >}})** — the
  people. Neighbours who said yes in advance: yes, wake me; yes, I'll go.
- **[The alarm]({{< relref "/technology" >}})** — one button that reaches all of
  them at once. A phone for those who have one; a wall button or a pendant for
  those who can't work one.
- **[The Protocol]({{< relref "/protocols" >}})** — observe and report, 062 or
  112 first, behaviour and vehicles never origins, and never intervene, pursue or
  detain.
- **[The signage]({{< relref "/resources" >}})** — a sign at the entrance, a
  plaque on a wall, a sticker in a window. Free to download and adapt.

Half the value isn't emergencies at all. A network that knows who lives alone,
and checks on them, cuts loneliness as well as risk — and nobody answering *is*
the alert.

## What it isn't

- **Not a patrol.** Citizen patrols have no legal cover in Spain, and every
  serious scheme in the world is built on being eyes, ears and hands.
- **Not a replacement for 062 and 112.** Every alert carries both numbers.
  Reaching the authorities is a solved problem; reaching the neighbour two
  hundred metres away is not.
- **Not a register of the vulnerable.** Escudo stores a name and a way to reach
  it, nothing more.

## Where it stands

- ☑ **Researched** — [what other countries do]({{< relref "/examples" >}}), what
  the evidence honestly supports, what Spanish law allows.
- ☑ **The alert layer is built and in use** — a button on Telegram and a village group,
  free, on any phone.
  [How it works]({{< relref "/technology/telegram" >}}).
- ☑ **Crest, signs and stickers designed** and
  [free to take]({{< relref "/resources" >}}).
- ☑ **The phone layer is live** — the village has a León number, and ringing it
  raises the group. An ordinary phone can now raise the alarm; a pendant or a
  wall unit can too, once a household has one.
  [How it works]({{< relref "/technology/devices" >}}).
- ◐ **This site is the proposal** to the Ayuntamiento and the Junta Vecinal,
  written in the open.
