# Printable documents

Setting up a Red Escudo rests on a few paper documents: the signage that goes to the printer, and the handouts given in person when someone signs up or when it helps to have the protocol in plain sight. This is where the print-ready files live.

## Ready to print

The signage designs are finished and available as vector original (SVG) and high resolution (PNG). The detail of each piece — where it goes, in what material — is in **[Signs and stickers]({{< relref "signs-and-stickers" >}})**.

| Document | Use | Files |
|---|---|---|
| **Sticker** | Window, door or letterbox of a member household | [SVG](/img/signage/en/escudo-sticker.svg) · [PNG](/img/signage/en/escudo-sticker-3200.png) |
| **Plaque** | Wall, corner or lamppost, durable outdoors | [SVG](/img/signage/en/escudo-plaque.svg) · [PNG](/img/signage/en/escudo-plaque-3200.png) |
| **Entrance sign** | Village approaches, road-sign format | [SVG](/img/signage/en/escudo-roadsign.svg) · [PNG](/img/signage/en/escudo-roadsign-3200.png) |

These carry `999 · 112`. The Spanish set, with `062 Guardia Civil · 112 Emergencias`, is also ready to print: [sticker](/img/signage/es/escudo-sticker.svg) · [plaque](/img/signage/es/escudo-plaque.svg) · [entrance sign](/img/signage/es/escudo-roadsign.svg). Put your own country's numbers on before printing either set.
