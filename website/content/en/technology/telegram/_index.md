# Telegram

The alert layer, built and in use. **One button, and the whole village knows
within a second.**

## Why Telegram

It's already on the phone, and most villages already have a group on it. Nobody
installs, learns or opens anything new during an emergency. It also brings, for
free:

- **Notifications that work**, including on locked phones.
- **Group messaging** with delivery, read state and history.
- **Identity** — people are already themselves in the group. No accounts, no
  passwords.
- **No cost**, on any phone, with nothing to bill a village for.

It depends on a company we don't control and on the internet being up. Both are
covered in
[what this layer doesn't solve]({{< relref "/technology/telegram/the-group-and-the-button" >}}#what-this-layer-doesnt-solve).

## Two elements

- **`Botón Escudo`** — the bot. A private chat with permanent buttons: the
  virtual panic button. Alerts are *raised* here.
- **`Red Escudo <Village>`** — the group. Alerts *arrive* here, and neighbours
  organise who goes.

Telegram allows neither permanent buttons nor location requests inside a group,
so raising and receiving happen in different places.
