The Dutch did what everyone's village group does — a WhatsApp group for the
neighbourhood — and then did the thing nobody does: they wrote down the rules.
Started in June 2015, formalised as Stichting WABP in 2017, and now **over 10,000
registered groups with around 750,000 participants**.

This is Escudo's closest relative, and where our protocol comes from.

## SAAR — four steps, in order

The protocol is one word, and everybody in the group knows it:

1. **S — Signaleer.** Notice, and get a good description of the situation.
2. **A — Alarmeer 112.** If police are needed, **call first**, before anything
   else. Then tell the group you've called, so they don't all ring too.
3. **A — App.** Share the observation with the group: where, what, description.
   Current incidents only.
4. **R — Reageer.** React **only if it's safe** — go outside, be visible, ask
   "can I help you?" from a distance, so whoever it is knows they've been seen.
   Never confront, never detain.

Alongside it, plain house rules: 18+, residents only, groups capped around fifty,
no discrimination, no taking the law into your own hands, no passing on members'
personal data, and be sparing with photos of people. Window and street stickers
do the deterrent work.

## What we take

- **SAAR itself**, translated one step over: call **062 or 112** rather than
  the Dutch numbers, and tell the group you've called.
  [Our version]({{< relref "/protocols/response" >}}).
- **Written rules before the first incident, not after it.**
- **The "react only from safety" clause**, word for word in spirit. Making
  yourself visible is the intervention. There is no other one.
- **Signage as deterrent** — the Dutch borden are the direct ancestor of our
  [village-entrance sign]({{< relref "/resources/signs-and-stickers" >}}).

## What we leave

- **The suspicion-first framing.** A Dutch group exists to report suspicious
  situations. A Red Escudo exists to get help to a neighbour; suspicious
  situations are one item on the list.
- **An open chat channel.** Ours is
  [a button for raising the alarm and a group for organising who goes]({{< relref "/technology/telegram" >}}) —
  narrow on purpose. Volume is the enemy.
- **Any complacency about the results.** The Dutch evidence is genuinely mixed,
  and the later studies are the important ones —
  [what the evidence says]({{< relref "evidence" >}}).
