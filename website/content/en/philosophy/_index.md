# Philosophy

Escudo is a way of organising neighbours, with technology in service of it. Three
convictions, and everything else on this site follows from them.

- **[The first minutes belong to the village]({{< relref "why-community-response" >}})**
  — help is coming, but from somewhere else. Whoever is already here decides how
  the next ten minutes go.
- **[Nobody owns it]({{< relref "horizontal-not-top-down" >}})** — no control
  room, no operator, no fee. Alerts travel sideways, and the village runs its own
  network, alongside the authorities and never around them.
- **[Elderly neighbours are participants, not a caseload]({{< relref "dignity-and-inclusion" >}})**
  — the hardest problem isn't coverage or batteries, it's a person deciding not
  to be a nuisance.

## What follows

- Observe and report — never intervene, pursue or detain
  ([the Protocol]({{< relref "/protocols/response" >}})).
- 062 or 112 first, always.
- Behaviour and vehicles,
  [never ethnicity]({{< relref "/protocols/response" >}}).
- A name and a way to reach it. No register of who is frail.
- Open by default: model, artwork, software, runbook.

## What we don't claim

Escudo will not make a village crime-free. Schemes like this show modest,
inconsistent effects on crime, and badly run ones leave people feeling *less*
safe. What they reliably deliver is faster reporting, visible deterrence and
neighbours who know each other —
[the honest evidence]({{< relref "/examples/evidence" >}}).
